# Appendix A3 — Session Notes

One section per participant. These are the notes that every `P#-Q#` evidence ID
in the report resolves to — so a marker can follow **P1-Q6** from a requirement
back to the sentence a real person said.

> **Fill this from real sessions.** Below is the template plus the question map.
> Write during the session, not after. Write **their** words, not your summary.

---

## How to take these notes

1. **Verbatim in quotation marks, paraphrase without.** If you didn't catch it
   exactly, don't put quotation marks on it. A paraphrase is still usable
   evidence; a paraphrase dressed as a quote is not.
2. **Write the misreadings down word for word.** When someone misreads a screen,
   *that is the finding*. It is the single most valuable thing you will write all
   session, and it evaporates in about ninety seconds.
3. **Mark surprises with `★`.** Anything that contradicts what you expected.
   Affinity mapping later is mostly a matter of collecting the stars.
4. **Note silences and hesitations, with the screen.** *"Paused 20 s on S-10"* is
   data. It is where **F-09** came from.
5. **Three lines immediately after, before you speak to anyone:** the best quote,
   the one thing that surprised you, and yes / no / maybe — would they actually
   use it.
