## Recruitment notes

- **Do not interview your project group.** Someone who helped build it cannot be
  surprised by it, and an examiner can tell.
- **Recruit at least one sceptic.** A report where everyone liked it looks like
  you only asked your friends. One person explaining why they would never lend
  their board is worth five polite yeses — and it is a *finding*, not a setback.
- **P6 and P7 are the hardest to get and the most valuable.** Most student
  projects have no SC-3 participant at all. A twenty-minute conversation with
  someone who runs a rental shack or a club store room is the difference between
  two stakeholder categories and three.
- **Run Strand A before Strand B** with the same person where possible, and note
  it — it inflates task success, which is a limitation you then state honestly in
  the report rather than one that quietly flatters your numbers.
